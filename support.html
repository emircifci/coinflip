Need help with the Quick Decision app? Find answers to common questions below or reach out to us directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock the Neon themes?

You can unlock the premium Neon themes for free by watching a short rewarded video ad. Once unlocked, the themes remain available during your session.

How can I change the app language?

You can change the language directly from the Home screen by tapping the Settings/Language icon in the top right corner. Currently, English and Turkish are supported.

Can I save my custom Spin Wheels?

Custom wheels are saved automatically using UserDefaults. You can create as many slices as you want and they will be there the next time you open the app.
